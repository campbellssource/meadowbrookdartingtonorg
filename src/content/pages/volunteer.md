# Volunteer for the DRA

  
  

  

The DRA is run by people who volunteer their free time. It’s a rewarding way to get to know people in the area and help to create something wonderful.

  
  

  

Volunteers help us run events like the annual extravaganza, staff the pool (when it’s open again) and with ad hoc requirements. 

If you are interested in becoming a Volunteer please sign up below. You’ll get emails a regular update about what has been happening and invites to take part.

You can also read our [volunteer policy](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1cEo2f4ofAHhfHUlE2IJzvft1AKN4UB6tgk6IdmNaDJY/edit?usp=sharing) for more detail. 

You can also just email [volunteer@meadowbrookdartington.org](mailto:volunteer@meadowbrookdartington.org?) if you have questions before completing the form.
