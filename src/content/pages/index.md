### About Meadowbrook

  
  

  

These 10 acres of land were generously bequeathed to the village by the Elmhirsts and Dartington Hall Trust in 1962, and are now managed by Dartington Recreation Association (DRA), a charity run by local volunteers, on a lease that next comes up for renewal in 2118!
