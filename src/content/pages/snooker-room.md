Full size snooker table with ample seating for spectators. Cues and balls provided. 

The snooker room is located upstairs in the main Meadowbook building.

You’ll receive a confirmation email after booking with the codes you’ll need to access the room..

Rules for snooker are:
- No food in the snooker room.
- Drinks purchased on the premises are allowed.
- The table must be covered, lights and any heaters turned off prior to leaving.
- The cost of repairing any damage caused to the table will be the responsibility of the user.
- Unaccompanied under 18's will not be allowed.

Please note that THH staff are not able to help with access.
Please ensure you lock the door when leaving and put the key back in the lock box. 
Could you please ensure the pass doors are closed when you leave, if there are no other people on the premises.
